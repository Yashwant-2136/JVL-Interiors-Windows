5. INTERACTIVE ENGINE & ALGORITHMIC SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. "Get Your Estimation" Calculation Logic:
   1. Base Cost Calculation (Area Sqft * Base Rate):
      - Interior Work: 1 BHK (600 sqft), 2 BHK (900 sqft), 3 BHK (1200 sqft), 4 BHK+ (1600 sqft).
      - Modular Kitchen: L-Shape (100 sqft), U-Shape (130 sqft), Straight (80 sqft), Parallel (120 sqft).
      - System Windows / Partition Works: Window Count * Avg Size (default 20 sqft).
      - False Ceiling: Room area selection (minimum 50 sqft).
   2. Material Premium:
      - Laminate / Standard: Baseline (+0%)
      - Acrylic / Teak / Premium: Multiplier applied (+15% to +35%)
   3. Add-on Services:
      - Lightning Protection, Thermal Insulation, Acoustic Proofing, Smart Locks (Flat fee increments).
   4. Tax & Range Calculations:
      - Subtotal (Actual Price Excl. Tax) = Base Cost + Material Premium + Add-ons
      - GST Tax Amount (18%) = Math.round(Subtotal * 0.18)
      - Total Amount (Incl. 18% Tax) = Subtotal + GST Tax Amount
      - Indicative Range Minimum = Total Amount
      - Indicative Range Maximum = Math.round(Total Amount * 1.15)
   5. Display Typography:
      - Estimated Cost Range numbers rendered in 'Calibri', 'Inter', sans-serif.

B. "Get Your Quotation" Engine:
   - Captures: Full Name, Phone, Email, Selected Service, City / Area.
   - Reference Generator: `JVL-YYYY-XXXX` (e.g., JVL-2026-4892).
   - Data Transfer: Serializes payload into `sessionStorage.jvl_quotation_data`.
   - Output: Automatically opens `invoice.html` in new tab or fallback in-page preview.
