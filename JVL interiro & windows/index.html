Interior Work

What you'll configure in your estimation:

• Space Details

Tell us your BHK/Layout type (1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK, 4 BHK+) and which rooms you want designed (Living Room, Kitchen/Pantry, Master Bedroom, Kids Bedroom, Guest Bedroom, Bathroom(s)) — so we scope the project to exactly what you need, not a flat whole-home price.

• Size

Enter your total project area in sqft. We'll pre-fill a sensible default based on your room selections, but you can adjust it to match your exact space.

• Finishes & Add-ons

Choose your finish tier: Laminate (budget-friendly), Acrylic (premium high-gloss), or PU Paint/Veneer (luxury seamless finish). You can also add optional services like electrical shifting, plumbing shifting, full house painting, false ceiling in major rooms, kitchen chimney & hob, and designer wooden accents.
